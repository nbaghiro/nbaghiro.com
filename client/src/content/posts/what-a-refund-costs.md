summary: The cost of a refund includes what happens to the original processing fee. A practical way to compare providers using completed sales and cancellations.

A full refund can return all of the customer's money while leaving the business with a processing cost.

[Stripe’s published policy](https://support.stripe.com/questions/understanding-fees-for-refunded-payments) says the processing fees from the original payment are not returned. Whether issuing the refund adds a separate fee depends on the pricing agreement.

## Comparing payment costs

That distinction matters when comparing payment providers. The rate on a completed sale only describes part of the cost. A business with frequent cancellations can have a different effective cost from one with the same sales volume and very few refunds.

For example, if a customer pays $100 and receives $100 back, the business has reversed the sale. If the original processing fee is retained, it still has an expense from that transaction.

I would include a realistic month of refunds in a pricing comparison, alongside completed sales. It gives a more useful estimate than multiplying every payment by the advertised rate.

## Comparing the full month

The useful comparison starts with a representative set of transactions. Keep the completed sales and the refunded sales separate, then apply each provider’s published terms to both. Include any fixed subscription charge in the same comparison. This is an estimate of processing costs, rather than a calculation of the business’s profit.

For a simple illustration, suppose a business takes 100 payments and refunds ten in full. A pricing comparison based only on the 90 retained sales would miss fees charged on those ten refunded payments if the provider keeps them. The refund rate is part of the input, even though the revenue from those transactions has been reversed.

## Reading the agreement

There are two separate questions to answer: what happens to the original fee, and whether making the refund incurs another charge. Different payment methods or negotiated agreements can have different terms. The published headline rate may not answer either question.

This also makes a useful review of the statement itself. Pick a completed sale and a refunded sale, follow each through the records, and check which charges remain. A small worked example makes the pricing agreement easier to understand before it is applied to a full month.
